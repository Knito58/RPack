Crafting Table:

	24 slabs from 3 logs (all vanilla wood types)
	16 stairs from 6 logs (all vanilla wood types)
	16 sticks from 2 logs (all vanilla wood types)
	4 chests from 8 logs
	4 quartz from 1 quartz block
	4 strings from 1 wool
	16 sticks from 2 logs
	full set of chainmail armor
	elytra
	spawner
	lodestone
	grass block
	mycelium block
	podzol block
	dispenser from dropper + bow
	coal block from charcoal
	4 cobwebs from a slimeball and 4 strings
	recycle saddle to 4 leather
	crimson nylium
	warped nylium
	slime spawn egg
	4 clay from clay block
	1 pointed dripstone from 5 dripstone blocks
	7 crying obsidian from 7 obsidian, ghast tear and lava bucket
	
	
Smithing Table:

	4 black dye from 1 + 1 charcoal (one left, one right)
	2 nuggets for a chain (one left, one right)
	repair chipped anvil with a nugget
	repair damaged anvil with an ingot (fixed in 1.2.9)
	- or -
	repair damaged anvil with a nugget and receive a chipped anvil
	
Furnace:

	leather from rotten flesh
	enderpearl from slime spawn egg
	recycle iron armor and sword to iron ingots
	recycle gold armor and sword to gold ingots
	recycle bucket to 3 iron ingots
	recycle horse armor to 8 of its type (even diamond)
	smooth basalt to dripstone block
	
	- Furnace gives xp according to count of ingots it produces
	- any enchantments are lost
	- all items count as new, damage value won't matter

How to install this:

	Old World:
	
	Put RPack zip file into the datapacks folder of your saved world and reload world.
	
	New World

	
	1.17 Fabric:
	
	You may install it using the "datapacks" - button. Works with drag & drop, too.
	Don't forget to move the pack from the left to the right side with 
	the arrow in the icon.
	
	Pre-1.17:

	Ignore the "datapacks" - button of the world creating GUI.
	When you like your new world, then shutdown Minecraft.
	Put the RPack-***.zip into the savegame\datapacks folder of your new world while the game is shut down. Start up everything from scratch.
	

Use JEI to look into the new recipes.


MC Versions

	Made for 1.17.x and 1.16.x (1.16.1, 1.16.2, 1.16.3, 1.16.4, 1.16.5 atm)
	RPack Versions < 1.2.4 should probably work with MC Version 1.14+ because of pack_format 5
	However, if it fails for your older MC then try to lower the number for "pack_format" in the pack.mcmeta
	accordingly to the following list of pack_format values and MineCraft versions.

pack_format values (source = minecraft.gamepedia.com)

    1 for versions 1.6.1 – 1.8.9
    2 for versions 1.9 – 1.10.2
    3 for versions 1.11 – 1.12.2
    4 for versions 1.13 – 1.14.4
    5 for versions 1.15 – 1.16.1
    6 for versions 1.16.2 – 1.16.5 (this is it for RPack 1.2.8)
    7 for versions 1.17+

Pack format info: https://minecraft.gamepedia.com/Tutorials/Creating_a_resource_pack#.22pack_format.22
