# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- Fixed: the black dye recipes were inconsistent. On the smithing table, any 2 coal or charcoal, in any mix, now give 4 black dye.
- New: 2 coal blocks give 40 black dye on the smithing table.

## 2.0.0

- Now a Fabric mod (needs Fabric API) for Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3, active in every world automatically.
- All recipes rewritten in the current recipe format.
- Fixed: iron and gold recycling, bucket and horse armor recipes now give the stated number of items and XP.
- Fixed: smithing table recipes (black dye, chain, anvil repair) work again; they had stopped working in 1.20.
- Fixed: gold horse armor recycling (wrong item ID before).
- Iron and gold tools, spears and chainmail armor now smelt back into their full ingot cost instead of one nugget.
- Removed: elytra, leather from rotten flesh, ender pearl from slime spawn egg.
- Slabs and stairs from logs now accept wood and stripped logs too, and cover mangrove, cherry, pale oak, crimson, warped and (26.3) poplar.
- All furnace recipes also work in the blast furnace.
